How to Play

  • WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump
  • Shift or double-tap W to sprint (drains hunger)
  • Left click break · Right click place or eat
  • E eat selected food · 1–9 hotbar · Esc menu
  • Leaves sometimes drop apples that restore hunger
  • Falls beyond 3 blocks hurt · empty hunger starves you
  • Pigs drop pork · zombies hunt at night and burn by day
  • Left click a mob to fight it · watch the Day counter up top
  • Creative: unlimited blocks, double-tap Space to fly
  • Hardcore: Hard difficulty, death deletes the world
